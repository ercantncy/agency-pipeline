# Reference Setup Agreement

**From:** Ercan Tuncay (hello@example.com)
**To:** [Agency Contact], [Agency Name]
**Date:** [Date]

Hi [Agency Contact],

Thanks for agreeing to be one of my first two Reference Setup agencies. I will build the Agency Reporting Pipeline for you at no charge. In return, I ask for an honest reference once it is live (details below). This letter sets out what each of us is committing to, in plain English.

## What I will deliver

- Up to 4 data sources, chosen from Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 and Search Console.
- A warehouse in your own BigQuery project, with the dbt models and tests that clean and reconcile the data.
- A white-label Looker Studio report template for up to 3 of your end clients.
- Monitoring alerts for stale data, broken tracking and sudden traffic drops.
- The agency monitoring web app, placed behind access control so only your team can see it.
- A recorded Loom handover and a short written handover note.
- One month of free maintenance after go-live: I fix anything I built that breaks.

## What is not included

- Sources beyond the 4 above.
- Custom dashboards for individual end clients beyond the shared template.
- Managing or optimising your ad accounts.
- Fixing tracking on your or your clients' websites. I will diagnose problems and tell you what to change; implementing the fixes can be quoted separately if you want it.
- Connector licence costs, if any are needed. I will tell you before anything is bought.

## What I need from you

- Read-only access to each source, typically: Google Ads read-only, GA4 Viewer, Meta Analyst through Business Manager partner access, and a restricted user in Search Console.
- A Google Cloud project with billing enabled, owned by your agency, so the data lives in your account.
- One point of contact on your side, who replies to questions and review requests within 3 business days.

## Data and ownership

Your data stays in your accounts. I will not copy it elsewhere beyond what is needed to build and test, and I will keep everything about your agency and your clients confidential. No end-client data or numbers will ever appear in my marketing. You own the BigQuery project, datasets, dashboards and the delivered models. I keep the right to reuse the generic code and templates (not your data or branding) in other work. When we finish, I will remove my access to all your accounts and confirm in writing.

## Timeline

My target is go-live within 3 weeks of receiving access.

- Week 1: access confirmed, sources connected, raw data landing.
- Week 2: models and tests running, template draft ready for your review.
- Week 3: monitoring and web app live, template finalised, Loom handover.

Delays in access or feedback move the dates by the same amount.

## What I ask in return

- A short written testimonial within 2 weeks of go-live.
- A case study, named or anonymised, as you prefer. You approve the final text before it is published.
- A 20-minute call in month 2 to talk about a paid Reporting Ops Retainer. There is no obligation to buy.
- I will mention your agency's name or logo only if you opt in, in writing.

## After month 1

You can (a) continue on the Reporting Ops Retainer, currently $1,500 per month for early clients; (b) take the handover and part ways, with the pipeline yours to run; or (c) pause and come back later. All are fine.

## Ending early

Either of us can end this at any time with written notice (email is fine). If that happens, I will hand over whatever is built so far, remove my access, and you owe nothing. Any testimonial or case study commitment lapses if I have not delivered a working setup. If you end it after go-live, I would still appreciate the testimonial, but it is not required.

## The small print

This letter is not a contract for paid services; any paid work would be agreed separately in writing. I will do the work in good faith and with reasonable care. My liability is limited to that good-faith effort, and I cannot guarantee specific business results, such as time saved or revenue gained.

Warm regards,
Ercan
